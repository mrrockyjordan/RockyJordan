About This Song

"Poddhugala Leshnama" (meaning "Did We Wake Up Early?") is Track 2 of Rocky Jordan's Album XII. Told in the same self-deprecating comedy style as "I Was A Loser," the track follows the daily struggle of trying to build discipline — early gym sessions, meal timings, weight gain frustration, deleted Instagram apps, and the constant internal fight between "start tomorrow" and actually starting. It's a gym-life anthem for anyone who's promised themselves a fresh routine a hundred times over.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Poddhugala Leshnama about?
A Telugu rap about the daily struggle to build gym discipline and healthy habits — early workouts, strict diets that fall apart by evening, and the endless cycle of promising to start "from tomorrow."
Is this part of Album XII?
Yes. This is Track 2 of Album XII, Rocky Jordan's series of 12 Telugu rap singles showcasing original writing and authentic hip-hop in Telugu.

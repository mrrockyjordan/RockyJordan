THE PERSON BEHIND THE BARS

TWO CITIES.
ONE VOICE.

A little humor. A lot to say.

Rocky Jordan writes Telugu rap from the life around him: work pressure, big ambitions, familiar excuses, and the urge to keep going. Hyderabad slang meets New York life; humor opens the door to harder questions about society, belief, and belonging.

The writing moves from conversational Telugu to tightly woven poetry, switching between Telugu, Hindi, and English. Album XII brings that range into 12 Telugu rap singles.
